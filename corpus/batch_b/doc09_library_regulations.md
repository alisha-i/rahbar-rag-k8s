# Central University Library Lending Regulations

## 1. Borrowing Privileges
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books concurrently for a duration of 14 calendar days. Graduate researchers may borrow up to 8 books for 30 calendar days. Reference books and journals cannot be taken outside the library.

## 2. Overdue Fines
A fine of Rs. 20 per day per book will be charged for items returned after the due date. Lost books must be replaced with the latest edition or reimbursed at double the purchase price.

## 3. Digital Repository
Access to IEEE Xplore and ACM Digital Library is accessible on campus Wi-Fi through IP authentication.
