# Margalla Institute of Technology: Academic Rules (2027 edition)

This edition formally updates and replaces the 2026 edition for all undergraduate students starting Fall 2027.

## 1. Attendance
1.1 A student must attend at least 85 percent of the lectures of each course (increased from 80% in 2026) to sit the final examination.

## 2. Academic Warning & Probation
2.1 A student whose cumulative grade point average (CGPA) falls below 2.20 at the end of a regular semester is placed on academic warning.
2.2 A student who remains on academic warning for two consecutive semesters is dropped from the university roll.
