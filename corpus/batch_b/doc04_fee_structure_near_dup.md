# University Fee Regulations and Payment Schedule

## 1. Tuition Fee Deadlines
Tuition payments for every semester must be deposited prior to the commencement of classes as notified by the Treasurer's Office. Late payment surcharge of Rs. 100 per day will be levied up to a maximum of 14 days after the deadline.

## 2. Refund Policy
If a candidate withdraws from the degree program within the first week of classes, 100% of the tuition fee is refundable. In the second week, 50% is refundable. After the second week, no tuition fee will be refunded under any circumstances.

## 3. Financial Aid
Merit scholarships are awarded to top 3 students of each department based on SGPA of the preceding semester. Need-based financial assistance is available upon committee interview.
